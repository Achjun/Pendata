Rumus prediksi:

\[ y = \frac{\sum (w_i \times y_i)}{\sum w_i} \]

Perhitungan:

\[ y = \frac{(2×0)+(2×1)+(0.894×0)+(2×1)+(2×0)+(0.894×1)} {2+2+0.894+2+2+0.894} \]
\[ y = \frac{4.894}{9.788} \]
\[ y \approx 0.5 \]
